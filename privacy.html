Privacy Policy

Morca Studio · last updated 1 November 2026

Morca Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Morca Stories and MORCA Earth to manage those channels. It is not offered to other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, Morca Studio uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload and schedule videos, set thumbnails and playlists, read and reply to comments, and read view and audience statistics of the owner's own channels.

Where data is kept

Access tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Morca Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.
